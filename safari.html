Safari planning

Plan your safari with less stress.

Golden Hut is a comfortable base for your Pilibhit Tiger Reserve trip. We can help you plan your stay, while permits and forest rules are handled by the Forest Department.

How it works

Your safari, without the confusion.

Wildlife trips are better when the essentials are clear before you leave the resort. Start with the official booking portal, then share your dates with us so we can help you plan the stay around the drive.

01 · Check

Confirm the current season, gate, permit availability and visitor rules.

02 · Book

Use the official UP Eco-Tourism or reserve portal for the applicable safari.

03 · Share

Tell Golden Hut your arrival, group size and preferred safari window.

04 · Return

Come back to the pool, lawn and a slower evening.

Travel responsibly

The reserve is a protected landscape.

Respect is part of the experience — for wildlife, local communities, guides and the forest staff who protect the area.

01

Keep distance

Observe animals quietly and never pressure a sighting.

02

Follow the route

Stay on authorised roads and follow your guide’s instructions.

03

Leave no trace

Carry waste back and do not feed or disturb wildlife.

04

Verify before travel

Check dates, closures, fees and permits on official portals.
