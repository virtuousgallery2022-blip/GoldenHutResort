Why Pilibhit?

A different kind of Indian wildlife escape.

Pilibhit brings together sal forest, tall grassland, wetlands and river landscapes in the Terai — a setting that rewards the curious traveller.

01

Terai country

Part of the Terai Arc Landscape in the upper Gangetic Plain, close to the Himalayan foothills and the India–Nepal border.

02

35 mammals

The reserve’s sal forests, grasslands and seasonal swamps support rich mammal diversity.

03

350 bird species

Bring binoculars: the reserve’s varied habitats support an exceptional birding experience through the year.

04

Chuka & Sharda

Chuka Beach and the 22-km Sharda Sagar Dam add water, light and a completely different rhythm to the forest holiday.
