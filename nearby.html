Nearby places

More to see around Puranpur.

Make your Golden Hut stay bigger than one safari. Explore forest roads, calm water, local history and quiet places around Pilibhit.

Easy day ideas

Choose a slower day between safaris.

These places are listed in district tourism information. Routes, entry and opening times can change, so please confirm locally before you leave.

Water and forest

Chuka Beach & Sharda Sagar

Enjoy open sky, water views and a quiet change of pace near the reserve. The Sharda Sagar Dam runs along the reserve boundary.

Reserve attraction

Bifurcation

A named attraction inside the reserve with open water and forest views. It is a good stop for photographs and fresh air.

Nature and birds

Sapt Sarovar & Birding Point

Look for water, trees and bird life. Keep your voice low, carry binoculars and follow local forest rules.

Learn the landscape

Nature Interpretation Centre, Mahof

A useful stop to learn about the reserve, its animals and its habitats before or after a safari.

Puranpur area

Raja Venu ka Teela

A small historic mound in Puranpur tehsil. The district tourism page places it about 1 km from Shahgarh railway station.

Pilibhit town

Gauri Shankar Temple

An old temple beside the Devha and Khakra rivers. The district tourism page describes it as around 250 years old and close to Pilibhit Railway Station.
